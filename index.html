Frequently asked questions

Common questions from contractors and suppliers before their first tender with us.

What is an e-tender filing service?

An e-tender filing service helps contractors and suppliers prepare and submit government tenders through official e-procurement portals — including document checking, technical and financial bid preparation, and submission before the deadline.

How can I submit a government tender online?

Most departments require registration on their e-procurement portal, a valid Digital Signature Certificate, and the tender's technical and financial documents uploaded in the required format before the deadline. We can guide you through each step.

Can you help with tender document checking?

Yes. We review your registration, PAN/GST, past-experience and financial documents against what the tender notice asks for, and flag anything missing or inconsistent before submission.

Do I need a DSC for e-tender submission?

Most e-procurement portals require a valid Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate to submit a bid. Requirements can vary by portal, so we confirm what your specific tender needs and help with applying for or renewing a DSC.

Can contractors from outside Agra use the service?

Yes. Document review, DSC assistance and bid submission are handled online, so contractors and suppliers based outside Agra can also use the service.

Do you provide technical bid assistance?

Yes. We help organise and format the technical bid — eligibility proof, experience certificates and technical specifications — according to the tender document's requirements.

Can you help with Nagar Nigam Agra tenders?

Yes. We support contractors and suppliers participating in Nagar Nigam Agra tenders with documentation, portal filing and bid submission.

Can you help with PWD tenders?

Yes. We assist with PWD tender documentation, technical and financial bid preparation, and submission on the relevant portal.

What documents are generally required for a tender?

Typically your firm registration or incorporation certificate, PAN and GST details, past work experience letters, solvency certificate and EMD payment proof. We confirm the exact list once we see the tender notice.

Does using this service guarantee I will win the tender?

No. We support the documentation and filing process; the tender award decision rests with the concerned department based on its own evaluation criteria.
